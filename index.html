Teacher's Quick Guide · how this works (1-minute read)

What this is: a free card maker. You pick classroom jobs, choose a look, and get print-ready cards on full sheets (1 per page), half sheets (2 per page), or quarter sheets (4 per page). Every look uses the Edutopia colors and prints cleanly.

Step 1, Pick your jobs: tap any card to add it. Pick the classics grabs a ready-made set of favorites. Search for something specific, or add a custom job for anything not on the list. Tap the pencil on a card to change its name or description.

Step 2, Design: type your class name and year, click a look, and choose a size. Click any words right on a card to retype them, click the picture to swap it for one of 150 pictures, 230 icons, 260 emoji, or your own image. Hover a card for buttons to reorder or remove it.

Step 3, Print or present: PDF opens the print window (choose Save as PDF, and turn on "Background graphics" so the colors print). Slideshow downloads a PowerPoint file that also opens in Google Slides. Present now shows one job per slide full screen on your board.

Saving: everything you do is saved in this browser on this device, so you can come back and change jobs at the start of a new term. There is nothing to sign in to. Start Over clears it all for a fresh class.
